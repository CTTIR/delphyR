# Round closed by mistake

Closing a round ends data collection for it. The software does not reopen a
closed round: later entries would mix two conditions of collection in one data
set, and the close is the point up to which every confirmed response is
guaranteed to be included.

## Recognise

- Participants report "The round is closed or its deadline has passed".
- *Rounds* shows the state Closed; the approval history shows who closed it,
  when and with which rationale.

Check first whether the round is closed or only past its deadline. A round in
state Open whose deadline has passed is not closed: the study lead can move
its deadline to a later time under *Rounds and analysis → Change the deadline*.

## First

1. Do not freeze the round until the study lead has decided. A frozen
   snapshot is immutable.
2. Establish what is in the round: under *Rounds and analysis*, in the review of the round, how many members submitted. Only submitted responses become
   research data; responses that were saved but not submitted do not.
3. Stop any reminder campaign for the round: cancel it with a rationale.

## Resume

The study lead chooses and records one of these in the study documentation
under deviations from the protocol:

- **Continue with what was submitted.** Freeze and analyse the round as it is.
  The report states the number of assigned and submitted members.
- **Collect again in a further round.** Freeze the closed round, then prepare
  a new round with the same item versions. Members who did not submit take
  part again only if the protocol allows a return after a missed round. Both
  rounds stay in the data with their own numbers; nothing is merged.
- **Amend the protocol** if the further round needs a higher maximum number of
  rounds: under *Protocol*, upload the complete new version, **Validate and
  preview exact protocol**, give the rationale and **Approve new protocol
  version**. It applies to rounds prepared afterwards.

Tell participants what happens with responses they saved but did not submit.

## Responsible

Study lead. The person who closed the round reports it; the operator has no
part in the decision.

## Evidence

The round's approval history, the audit trail, the recorded deviation and the
message sent to participants.
