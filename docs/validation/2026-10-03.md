## Defects found and fixed while preparing this report

- A study without any round showed an error instead of its management
  section; the same construction gave a panel member without a round the
  wrong message.
- An input that was not ready could reach the failure message of a section
  and the technical log.
- The editorial section did not offer a round frozen in another section until
  it was refreshed by hand.
- The list of rounds started with the first round instead of the round due.
- The focus did not reach the heading of a newly shown block.
- The read-only display of a chosen file had no name; a checksum of 64
  characters forced sideways scrolling at 320 pixels.
- A freshly started application process served the files of its page only
  after rendering it once, so a page delivered by another process stayed
  blank; with two processes the first sign-in after the gateway changed its
  routing failed.
- An expired sign-in was reported as a general failure.
- The invitation refusal carried no reference.
- Nothing kept a restarted worker from delivering messages that waited when a
  backup was taken.
- The fixture scripts created private files with the default mode before
  restricting them.

## Not executed

- A test with a screen reader, with Safari, on a mobile device, and a statement
  of accessibility conformance.
- Production hosting with TLS, the institution's identity provider, central
  logout, MFA and secret management.
- A real message provider; bounces and complaints.
- A penetration test by an independent party.
- Point-in-time recovery, recovery objectives, the sign-in through the gateway
  on a restored instance.
- More than 50 sessions, several large studies at once, a database on another
  host, a wide-area network.
- A pilot with real users and the acceptance decision.
